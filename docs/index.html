3DSCalculator documentation

3DSCalculator computes the three-dimensional diffuse intensity I(h,k,l) of atomistic models (RMCProfile, LAMMPS, Scatty or unified HDF5 files) for X-rays, neutrons and electrons, and shows it as 3D slice planes, isosurfaces and 2D maps. It runs in the browser: structure files never leave your machine.

Guide The interface and the workflow: loading structures, the grid and scattering settings, the views, comparing with data and exports. Examples Worked examples with the shipped files: LiFeO₂, CaTiO₃, PMN, MnO and Fe spins, and the Pt chains of KCP. Theory The equations behind the calculator: total and diffuse intensity, magnetic scattering, normalization and Laue symmetrization. Supplementary Backend provenance, scattering-table families and reproducibility notes. Bibliography Formal references for the calculation, the scattering tables, RMC and the software the calculator uses. Troubleshooting Common issues and fixes: WebGPU, results that differ between backends, slow runs and files that are not recognized.
Precision notice: the WebGPU FFT/NUFFT stack and the CPU FFT path use fp32 arithmetic. For important scientific checks, compare against the Exact sum backend on a small grid.
